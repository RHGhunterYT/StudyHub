SYSTEM MANUAL v1.0

1. MINE BYTES: Click the main button to generate Bytes manually. Use Bytes to buy normal upgrades that increase your Bytes Per Second (BPS) and Bytes Per Click (BPC).

2. REBIRTH: Once you reach the target Bytes, initiate a Rebirth. This wipes your current run but grants Rebirth Points and increases your maximum upgrade capacity!

3. REBIRTH UPGRADES: Spend Rebirth Points on powerful permanent multipliers. These do NOT reset during a normal Rebirth.

4. ULTRA REBIRTH: Max out absolutely every normal AND rebirth upgrade to unlock the Ultra Rebirth. This resets EVERYTHING but shows that you've completed the entire game atleast once.
